Computer Science & Engineering Department

About Department

The Computer Science & Engineering Department provides quality technical education in Programming, Database Management System, Computer Networks, Operating Systems, Software Engineering, Web Technologies, Cloud Computing and Emerging Technologies.


Students gain theoretical knowledge together with practical laboratory experience through projects, seminars, industrial visits, technical workshops and skill-based learning.


Vision

To become a leading Computer Science & Engineering Department by providing quality technical education and producing competent engineers with innovation, leadership, professional ethics and lifelong learning.

Mission

• Provide quality technical education.

• Develop programming and analytical skills.

• Encourage innovation, research and teamwork.

• Prepare students for industry, entrepreneurship and higher education.
